# Shadmin-Islam-Prova

I graduated from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research uses Sentinel-1 radar satellite data to map cyclone damage in coastal Bangladesh. I also work on floods, coastal hazards, and MCDA.


Recent Publication

I have a peer-reviewed study in Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences (Q1, IF 4.7, 2026). It uses SAR coherence change detection to map cyclone damage in coastal Bangladesh. The study comes from my MS thesis. If you are interested in the study, read the paper here: https://doi.org/10.5194/nhess-26-5103-2026

My Research Interests
1. SAR and InSAR remote sensing
2. coherence and time series analysis
3. Flood hazard modeling and SAR flood mapping
4. Coastal salinity
5. ML for Earth observation
